# Explanation

Every Provider Analytics v1 explanation contains:

- a stable code;
- a kind: `strength`, `limitation`, or `observation`;
- a deterministic rule ID;
- a human-readable message;
- evidence references such as cohort, metric, sample, or report identifiers.

Explanations are generated from scorecard dimensions and coverage gaps. They do not introduce facts
that are absent from the evidence. The analytics output always states that no universal provider
score was calculated.
